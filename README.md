## About

Fifth semester of AI Engineering at FIME, UANL. Since 2025 I've worked at the
UANL University Hospital, where I lead **hu-ai-onco**, a mammography decision-support
platform that runs on top of the hospital PACS.

On the side I build for MasterCreators, run my own agency since high school, and
founded **GEIIA**, my faculty's AI student group, now 350 members.

Most of what I work on is private. Client contracts on one side, hospital data on
the other. What's below is what I can point at.

## Work

### Clinical AI · Hospital Universitario "Dr. José E. González", UANL

| Project | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **hu-ai-onco** | BI-RADS density grading and mass detection in mammography, wired into the hospital PACS. Ten services in production, open file with COFEPRIS. I lead a team of eight. | Private · in production |
| **SCT Viewer** | Clinical front end over Spinal Cord Toolbox. Compiled ANTs natively for ARM64 and took segmentation from ~10 min to ~10 s. Runs on five radiology workstations. | Private · in production |
| **DigitizerNet** | Turns a photographed 12-lead ECG back into signal. 11-stage auditable pipeline with biophysical verification, built for regulatory review. | Private · research |
